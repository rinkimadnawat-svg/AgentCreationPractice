# BrightBite Foods business brief

Leadership is reviewing product feedback before deciding the next-quarter response. Feedback arrives in Excel from retail stores, distributors, customer service and surveys. The file is inconsistent and management currently relies on manual summaries.

The product should help a business manager identify urgent product issues, compare products, regions and channels, inspect evidence, assign a suggested action, and download a management-ready Excel pack.

## Guardrails
- synthetic data only
- transparent rules, not causal claims
- no external APIs
- no automatic email, deployment, commit or publishing
- human approves requirements, architecture, code changes and release
